## What this site is

Sauda Shastra (सौदा शास्त्र, “the science of the trade”) is a free, independent knowledge site. It explains how Indian and global markets work for intraday traders, options traders and long-term investors, and it keeps a researched library of courses and books. Every guide names the primary documents it rests on, with the date each was read; every changeable number renders from the site's [verified facts register](../facts/) with the date it was last checked.

## Disclaimer

Educational content, not investment advice. The author is not a SEBI-registered research analyst or investment adviser. Derivatives trading carries risk of loss.

Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security, derivative or fund, and no page names a live instrument, target or stop. Figures are illustrative worked examples with their sources and the date each was verified.
